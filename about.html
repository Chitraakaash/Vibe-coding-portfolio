About

I'm a visual designer based in Bareilly, India. My creative journey spans digital illustration, character design, and UI/UX—transitioning from traditional print to product-focused design with intentionality.

Currently, I work as a Prepress Executive Design Operator at Parksons Packaging Ltd in Daman, adapting artwork into production-ready designs for pharmaceutical cartons, specialty packaging, and print materials at scale.

Working close to manufacturing has shaped how I approach design—balancing aesthetics with technical feasibility, precision, and real-world constraints. I approach each project with curiosity and respect for craft.

Before this, I spent two years at N P Construction Co. leading visual identity projects and marketing collateral, creating materials that resulted in a 30% increase in client engagement.
